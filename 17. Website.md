Adaptive Visual Dynamics (AVD) is an emerging technical standard that transforms correctable optometric parameters into adaptive visual rules for digital interfaces. Its goal is to improve readability and accessibility through dynamic adjustments of text size, contrast, spacing, and visual density.
AVD is an open specification, documented in both English and Spanish, designed to be seamlessly integrated into web and mobile systems, digital readers, and accessibility tools. The project is published under the MIT License and is fully prepared for future extensions, prototyping, and formal standardization processes.
